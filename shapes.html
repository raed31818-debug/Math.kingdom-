📐 Shapes & Measurement

Learn 2D and 3D shapes — area, volume, and units! (mm · cm · m)

🔷 2D Shapes (Flat Shapes)

Square

Area = side × side

All 4 sides equal

Rectangle

Area = length × width

Opposite sides equal

Circle

Area = π × r²

π ≈ 3.14, r = radius

Triangle

Area = ½ × base × height

3 sides, 3 angles

🧊 3D Shapes (Solid Shapes)

Cube

Volume = side³

All 6 faces are equal squares

Sphere

Volume = ⁴⁄₃ × π × r³

Like a ball

Cylinder

Volume = π × r² × h

Like a can of soda

📏 Units of Measurement

Length

• mm = millimetre (smallest)

• cm = centimetre (10 mm = 1 cm)

• m = metre (100 cm = 1 m · 1000 mm = 1 m)

Area (cm², m²)

• Area is measured in square units

• 1 cm² = 100 mm² · 1 m² = 10,000 cm²

Volume (cm³, m³)

• Volume is measured in cubic units

• 1 cm³ = 1000 mm³ · 1 m³ = 1,000,000 cm³
