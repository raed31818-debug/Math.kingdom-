🍎 What Is Multiplication?

Multiplication is a shortcut for adding the same number over and over.

3 × 4 means 3 groups of 4

🍎🍎🍎🍎
🍎🍎🍎🍎
🍎🍎🍎🍎

Count them all: 4 + 4 + 4 = 12, so 3 × 4 = 12! 🎉

💡 Cool Tricks

✋ The 9× Hand Trick

Hold up both hands. For 9 × 4, bend your 4th finger. Count fingers before (3) and after (6). The answer is 36!

🔟 Multiplying by 10

Just add a zero! 7 × 10 = 70.

✌️ Multiplying by 2

Just double the number! 6 × 2 = 12.

🖐️ Multiplying by 5

The answer always ends in 0 or 5.

🔢 Multiplying by 11

For 11 × 3 = 33. For 11 × 4 = 44. Just repeat the digit!

👨‍👩‍👧 For Parents & Teachers

Guidance and tips for supporting every kind of learner.

💜 Supporting Children with Special Needs

🕐 Go at Their Pace

Never rush. Five minutes of happy learning beats thirty minutes of frustration.

🎯 Short Sessions

10–15 minute sessions with breaks work best.

🔊 Use Sound & Visuals

Pair numbers with pictures, sounds, and movement.

✅ Celebrate Every Win

Even small progress deserves praise.

🧘 Watch for Overload

If your child gets restless, use the Calm Corner.

🧩 Adaptations for Different Needs

For ADHD

  • Short sessions (5–10 minutes)
  • Movement breaks between questions
  • Calm Mode reduces distractions

For Autism Spectrum

  • Predictable structure — same layout each time
  • Calm Mode reduces animation
  • Sound can be turned off

For Dyslexia

  • Large, high-contrast numbers
  • Visual groups (dots) help understanding
  • Voice narration reinforces learning

For Down Syndrome

  • Simple one-step instructions
  • Frequent positive reinforcement
  • Repeat the same level as needed
