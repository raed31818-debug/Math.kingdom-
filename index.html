🍎 What Is Multiplication?

Multiplication is a shortcut for adding the same number over and over.

3 × 4 means 3 groups of 4

🍎🍎🍎🍎
🍎🍎🍎🍎
🍎🍎🍎🍎

Count them all: 4 + 4 + 4 = 12, so 3 × 4 = 12! 🎉

💡 Cool Tricks

✋ The 9× Hand Trick

Hold up both hands. For 9 × 4, bend your 4th finger. Count fingers before (3) and after (6). The answer is 36!

🔟 Multiplying by 10

Just add a zero! 7 × 10 = 70.

✌️ Multiplying by 2

Just double the number! 6 × 2 = 12.

🖐️ Multiplying by 5

The answer always ends in 0 or 5. 5 × 3 = 15, 5 × 4 = 20.

🔄 Flip It!

3 × 4 = 4 × 3. You can swap the numbers and get the same answer!

👨‍👩‍👧 For Parents & Teachers

Guidance and tips for supporting every kind of learner.

💜 Supporting Children with Special Needs

Every child is unique. Here are some gentle strategies that help:

🕐 Go at Their Pace

Never rush. Five minutes of happy learning beats thirty minutes of frustration.

🎯 Short Sessions

10–15 minute sessions with breaks work best for most young learners.

🔊 Use Sound & Visuals

Pair numbers with pictures, sounds, and movement. Multisensory learning sticks.

✅ Celebrate Every Win

Even small progress deserves praise. Confidence grows with celebration.

🧘 Watch for Overload

If your child gets restless, use the Calm Corner. Learning needs a calm brain.

🔄 Repetition Is Magic

Repeat the same table many times before moving on. Mastery comes from familiarity.

🧩 Adaptations for Different Needs

For ADHD

  • Very short sessions (5–10 minutes)
  • Use movement breaks between questions
  • Enable Calm Mode to reduce distractions

For Autism Spectrum

  • Predictable structure — same layout each time
  • Calm Mode reduces animation
  • Sound can be turned off if preferred

For Dyslexia

  • Large, high-contrast numbers
  • Visual groups (dots) help understand quantity
  • Audio feedback reinforces learning

For Down Syndrome

  • Simple one-step instructions
  • Frequent positive reinforcement
  • Pair with physical objects (count real apples!)

For Anxiety / Sensory Sensitivity

  • Calm Corner for regulation
  • No timers or pressure
  • Soft colours and gentle sounds

📅 Suggested Learning Plan

  1. Week 1: Explore the home page, Calm Corner, and learn page together.
  2. Week 2: Play level 1 (×1) and level 2 (×2) with lots of breaks.
  3. Week 3: Add level 3 (×3). Use flashcards for review.
  4. Week 4: Try the Memory Match activity together.
  5. Week 5+: One new level per week, always reviewing older ones.
  6. Anytime: Use the Calm Corner whenever needed.

📞 When to Seek Extra Support

If your child is consistently frustrated, avoidant, or distressed around learning, talk to:

  • A paediatrician or family doctor
  • School special education coordinator
  • A child psychologist or educational therapist
  • Local learning support groups
